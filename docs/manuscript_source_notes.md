## Claims the data supports

- [RQ1] At low shot budgets (S=64) the simulated quantum-inspired retrieval increases hallucination relative to exact cosine, with the generator fixed (Holm-significant at N=all). (evidence: N-T-c rows; Table T5c; strength: moderate)
- [RQ1] The hallucination effect is mediated by retrieval: outcomes split almost entirely by whether the gold paragraph reached the context. (evidence: mediation table / F9; strength: moderate)
- [RQ1] At S >= 1024 no difference in hallucination is detectable with 200 questions. (evidence: T-c, T7d; strength: weak-moderate (absence of evidence at this n))
- [RQ2] The emulated estimator gives no retrieval-latency benefit: the shortlist arm executes the exact search first, and full-scan estimation is slower than Flat. (evidence: T4b; strength: strong)
- [RQ2] Over HTTP the query embedding and service hops dominate retrieval time for both arms; generation dominates the whole request. (evidence: T4c; strength: strong)
- [RQ3] At a fixed shot budget the Hit@3 gap is larger at the full corpus than at 1k paragraphs, and the shots needed for indistinguishable Hit@3 grow with N. (evidence: T3, shot thresholds; strength: moderate)
- [RQ3] The Tier B emulator reproduces the Tier A circuit statistics (mean, variance, 1/sqrt(S) error scaling). (evidence: T6, F2; strength: strong on mean / variance; weaker on distribution shape)
- [RQ4] Exact Flat retrieval is on the Pareto front at every corpus size; the quantum-sim arm can at best match it; IVF is the practical latency option. (evidence: T7b, T7c, F7, F7b; strength: moderate)
- [Method] The SWAP-test top-3 differs from the exact top-3 mainly for queries whose gold paragraph has a small score margin; a measurable share of top-1 results changes across seeds even at 1024 shots. (evidence: T8b, failure analysis; strength: moderate)

## Claims the data does NOT support (do not write these)

- [Speed] Any quantum speedup, advantage, or lower latency. The arm is a classical simulation; modelled shots and gates are counts, not time.
- [Hardware] Anything about real quantum hardware, noise, QRAM or state-preparation cost.
- [Generality] Results for other corpora (multi-hop, domain-specific), other embedders, or larger / better-calibrated generators (single dataset, embedder, 1.5B model).
- [Scale] Latency or memory scaling beyond about 21k paragraphs; the exponent CIs overlap between arms.
- [Cloud] Performance of a real cloud deployment (containers emulated on one shared laptop; free-tier cloud rerun not done).
- [Faithfulness] Validated faithfulness: the HHEM judge was not checked by humans (audit pending) and agrees only weakly with the NLI cross-check.
- [Equivalence] That the quantum-sim arm is equivalent to classical at S >= 1024; margins are post hoc and n=200.
- [Zardini] Any result for the Zardini-style quantum k-NN estimator (not implemented, D-3.3); Q-L2S and other extensions (E1-E5) were not run.

## Terminology

Define once (FINDINGS section 3): quantum-inspired retrieval = a retrieval stage whose similarity scores come from classically simulating the SWAP test, including finite-shot statistics. No quantum hardware, no speedup.
